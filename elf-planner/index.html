Tips that save your evenings

Set a phone alarm for 9 pm so the elf never forgets to move. On busy nights, just move the elf to a funny new spot; that counts. Keep a "backup bag" with candy canes, sticky notes and string for nights you forgot. If the elf does forget, a quick note saves it: "I stayed to watch you, you were so kind yesterday!" Keep small items and candy out of reach of kids under 3.
