How it is worked out

Gas = miles / MPG x gas price. Car wear is what each mile costs you beyond gas; $0.15 to $0.30 is typical for a small used car. Mileage deduction uses the IRS standard rate ($0.725 per business mile for 2026; check each January). The tax set-aside is your % of (pay minus the mileage deduction). Self-employment tax alone is about 14.1% of that. This is a planning helper, not tax advice.
