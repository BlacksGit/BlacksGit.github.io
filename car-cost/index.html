How it is worked out

Gas = miles / MPG x gas price. Value lost per year is a share of what the car is worth now: about 15% for a car up to 3 years old, 12% at 4 to 7, 10% at 8 to 12 and 7% after that. Tires last about 50,000 miles, so a set is spread over those miles. Maintenance and repairs default to about $1,000 a year for a mid-age car; raise it for an older car. The car payment is counted in full here; the interest part is the real cost, the rest pays down the loan. A planning estimate.
