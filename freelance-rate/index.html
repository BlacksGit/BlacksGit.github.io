How it is worked out

Billable hours = hours per week x billable share x (52 - weeks off). Admin, sales, email and learning are the unbilled share; 60 to 70% billable is typical for solo freelancers. The tool finds the yearly revenue where, after fees, costs, self-employment tax (15.3% on 92.35% of profit, the Social Security part stops at $184,500 for 2026), federal income tax (2026 single brackets, $16,100 standard deduction, 20% QBI deduction, half of SE tax deducted) and state tax, you keep your target plus retirement saving. Health insurance is treated as a cost here; self-employed health premiums are often deductible, which lowers the tax a little. An estimate for a single filer, not tax advice.
