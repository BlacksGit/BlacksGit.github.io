Snowball or avalanche?

Both pay every minimum and put all extra money on one debt. When it is paid off, its whole payment rolls to the next one. Snowball goes smallest balance first, so you clear debts sooner and stay motivated. Avalanche goes highest interest rate first, which always costs the same or less interest. If the gap shown above is small, pick the one you will stick with. Interest is worked out monthly at APR / 12. Fees, promo rates and changing rates are not included.
