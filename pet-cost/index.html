Where the numbers come from

Middle-of-the-road US prices for 2026. Food scales with size; premium food roughly doubles it. Routine vet covers a yearly exam and vaccines; preventives are flea, tick and heartworm for dogs, flea and tick for cats. The first year adds adoption or purchase, spay or neuter, first vaccines and starter supplies. Insurance is a typical accident-and-illness plan for a young pet. The emergency fund target is what one surprise vet visit often costs. Your area and your pet's breed can move these a lot.
