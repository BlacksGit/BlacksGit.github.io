How it counts

Tree: 50, 100 or 180 mini lights per foot of height. Strands are 100-light sets. Roofline: C9 bulbs 1 per foot in 25-foot strings, mini lights 100 per 22 feet, icicle 150 per 7.5 feet. Bushes: 150 lights each. Windows: about 15 feet of mini lights each. Power: LED mini 0.07 W, old-style mini 0.4 W, LED C9 0.6 W, old-style C9 7 W per bulb. Circuits: 15 amp at 120 V, loaded to 80% (1440 W).
