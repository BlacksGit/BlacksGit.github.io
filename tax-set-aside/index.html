How it is worked out

Profit on this payment = payment minus your business-cost %. Self-employment tax = 92.35% of profit x 15.3% (Social Security part stops at $184,500 of earnings in 2026). Federal income tax is figured on your whole expected year: profit minus half of SE tax, minus the 2026 standard deduction ($16,100 single, $32,200 married jointly, $24,150 head of household), minus the 20% qualified business income deduction, run through the 2026 brackets. That gives your average rate, which is applied to this payment. State tax is your % of profit.
