Fees used (US, 2026)

eBay: 13.6% of the total (item + shipping charged) + $0.40 per order, most categories.
Poshmark: 20%, or a flat $2.95 when the sale is under $15.
Mercari: 10% selling fee + 2.9% + $0.50 payment processing.
Depop (US): 3.3% + $0.45 payment processing.
Facebook Marketplace: 10% on shipped orders, $0.80 minimum. Local pickup is free.
Whatnot: 8% + 2.9% + $0.30.
Etsy: 6.5% transaction + 3% + $0.25 processing + $0.20 listing.
Fees change and some categories differ. Check your platform before you rely on a number.
