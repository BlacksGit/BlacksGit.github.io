PrintTally

3D print cost calculator, print log and spool inventory for Android.

What it does

  • Open a G-code or Bambu/Orca 3MF and get grams and print time filled in
  • Price breakdown: filament, electricity, printer wear, your time, extras and a failure allowance
  • Marketplace fee presets (Etsy, eBay, Shopify) built into the price, not taken out of your margin
  • Spool inventory that counts down as you log prints
  • Monthly prints, filament used, sales, profit and failure rate; CSV export

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026.

PrintTally does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting SDKs, and it does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

Your spools, print log and rates are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Android's own device backup may include this data if you have backup turned on; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by PrintTally.

When you open a G-code or 3MF file, the app reads it on the device to find the filament weight and print time. The file is not copied or uploaded.

"Share" and "Export CSV" open the Android share sheet. Where that text or file goes is your choice.

Uninstalling the app or using "Erase all data" in Settings deletes everything from the device.
