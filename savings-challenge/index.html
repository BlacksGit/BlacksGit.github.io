Make a savings challenge for your goal

The classic 52-week challenge always ends at $1,378. Type your own goal and date to get a week-by-week plan that adds up to exactly that. Tap a week when you have saved it. Your progress stays on this device.

See the Savings Challenge Tracker

Growing: the first week is small and each week adds the same step, like the classic challenge. Reverse: the big weeks come first, which helps if the end of your plan lands near the holidays. Amounts are rounded to whole dollars and the last week takes up the difference so the total is exact.
