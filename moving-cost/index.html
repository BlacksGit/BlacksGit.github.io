How rough is this?

These are rough 2026 US averages to compare options, not quotes. Truck: rental base plus a per-mile charge, gas at about 10 MPG, boxes and supplies, and paid helpers for loading at each end (two to four, by home size). Container: about one container per two bedrooms, priced by distance, plus helpers. Movers: local moves are priced by the hour for a crew; long moves by weight and distance. Prices swing a lot with season (summer and month-ends cost more), stairs and how far you are from a big city. Get at least three quotes, and check that long-distance movers have a USDOT number.
