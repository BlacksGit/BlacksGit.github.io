What the numbers mean

Cash flow = rent minus vacancy, repairs, big-ticket savings (roof, HVAC, water heater), management, tax, insurance, HOA, utilities and the loan payment. Cash-on-cash = yearly cash flow / cash you put in (down payment + closing and repairs). Cap rate = yearly income after costs but before the loan / price; it compares properties no matter how they are financed. Break-even rent is the rent where cash flow is $0. The 1% rule (monthly rent at least 1% of price) is a quick first filter, not a verdict. Not financial advice.
