How to play

Give everyone a card and something to mark squares with (candy, coins, crayons). The caller picks pictures one at a time with the phone caller or by cutting up the caller sheet. Players mark the square if they have it. First to get a full row, column or diagonal shouts BINGO. For a longer game play "four corners" or "full card". Every card in a set is different, and each card has a small number in the corner so you can check a winner against the caller.
