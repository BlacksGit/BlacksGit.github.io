Nestling

A calm, private baby tracker for Android.

What it does

  • One-tap breastfeeding timer with left/right switching and next-side suggestion
  • Bottle, pumping, solids, diaper, sleep and notes
  • "Last fed" countdown, today's totals and a 7/14-day daily rhythm chart
  • Night mode: dim red on black for 3am feeds
  • CSV export to share with your pediatrician

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026.

Nestling does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting SDKs, and it does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

Everything you log (feeds, diapers, sleep, notes, your baby's name and birthday) is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Android's own device backup may include this data if you have backup turned on; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by Nestling.

When you use "Export CSV", the app creates a file and opens the Android share sheet. Where that file goes is your choice.

Uninstalling the app or using "Erase all data" in Settings deletes the log from the device.

Nestling is a record-keeping tool, not medical advice. Contact your doctor with any concerns about your baby's health.
