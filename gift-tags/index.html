Christmas gift tag maker

Type the names once and print a full sheet of To/From tags. Santa's tags get their own style, so the kids won't spot your handwriting.

12 tags per page. Spare spots print with a blank To line for handwriting. Cut them out, punch the dot and tie with ribbon, or tape them flat. Your names stay on this device.

Ideas for Santa gift tags

  • Use a different wrapping paper for Santa's gifts that the kids have never seen.
  • Print Santa tags here so the writing doesn't match yours.
  • Add the "From the Elf" tag to a small gift on December 1 to start the season.
  • Keep one roll of tape and the tags hidden with the gifts, not in the kitchen drawer.
