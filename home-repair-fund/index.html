Where the numbers come from

Lifespans and costs are typical US figures for a mid-size home: asphalt roof about 25 years, furnace 18, central AC or heat pump 15, tank water heater 11, exterior paint 10, fridge 13, washer 11, dryer 13, dishwasher 10. Costs vary a lot by region and size, so use a quote if you have one. Each item is saved for over the years it has left. An item past its typical life is shown as due now and saved for over the next 12 months. Routine upkeep (gutters, caulk, small plumbing and electrical, service calls) uses the common 1% of home value rule.
