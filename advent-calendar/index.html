Advent activity calendar maker

One small family Christmas activity for every day until Christmas Eve. Quick ones on school nights, bigger ones on weekends. Swap any day you don't like, tick them off as you go, and print a calendar plus cut-out cards for your advent boxes or envelopes.

Kids in the house

Options

Your advent calendar

Tick a day when you've done it. Swap gives you a different idea that fits the same day.

Tips

Put each daily card in a numbered envelope, a paper bag or an advent box, and let the kids open one each morning so they know what's happening that day. The first day is decorating the tree and Christmas Eve is Santa night if you keep those turned on. The shopping list shows what to buy ahead. Your calendar is saved on this device only.
