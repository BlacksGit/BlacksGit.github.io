Christmas gift scavenger hunt maker

Turn one present into a treasure hunt. Tick the spots you have at home, pick the clue style, and print the cards. You also get a hide list that tells you exactly where each card goes, so nothing gets mixed up.

Clue style

Where can clues hide? Tap to turn spots on or off

Your hunt

Card 1 goes straight to your kid. Every other card hides at the spot the clue before it points to. Tap Swap to change a spot.

Prints 4 cards per page, then a hide list for you. Cut along the borders. Color printing looks best but black and white works.

Tips for a smooth hunt

Hide the cards after the kids are in bed, using the hide list in order. Little kids: walk with them and read the card out loud, the big picture is the real clue. Readers and big kids can go on their own. Skip anything hot or high up. For siblings, make one hunt per kid with different spots, or let them solve the same hunt as a team. Your choices are saved on this device only.

All 27 Christmas scavenger hunt clues

Every clue the maker uses, if you'd rather copy them by hand. Rhymes suit readers about 6 to 8, riddles suit 9 and up.

🎄 Christmas tree

Rhyme: I'm green and I sparkle with lights all aglow. Look under my branches, down low, down low!
Riddle: I wear lights and shiny balls, but I'm not going to a party. Search my branches.

🥛 Fridge

Rhyme: Where the milk stays cold and the leftovers sit, open the door: your next clue is in it!
Riddle: I hum all day, I light up when you open me, and I keep the milk cold.

🧊 Freezer

Rhyme: Brrr, it's chilly where the ice cream lives. Grab the clue that the cold spot gives!
Riddle: I'm colder than the North Pole and full of frozen treats.

🧺 Washing machine

Rhyme: Where dirty clothes spin round and round, your next clue is waiting to be found!
Riddle: I spin and splash but never get dizzy. Clothes go in dirty and come out clean.

🛁 Bathtub

Rhyme: Splish and splash, where the bubbles go. Peek inside: it's dry, you know!
Riddle: I hold lots of water, but you sit in me instead of drinking from me.

🛏️ Bed

Rhyme: Before you sleep, you climb up here. Look underneath: the clue is near!
Riddle: I have a head but never think, and feet but never walk. You visit me every night.

💤 Pillow

Rhyme: Soft and fluffy, under your head. Look beneath it, there on your bed!
Riddle: Every night I hold your head and keep your dreams company.

📚 Bookshelf

Rhyme: Stories and pictures, page after page. The next clue waits where the books are on stage!
Riddle: We have spines but no bones, and lots of stories but no floors.

🛋️ Couch

Rhyme: Where the family sits for a movie night, dig in the cushions, look left and right!
Riddle: I have arms but can't hug, and a back but can't stand up. Dig into my cushions.

👟 Shoes

Rhyme: Left and right, they go on your feet. Check inside the pair you wear down the street!
Riddle: We always come in pairs, and we have tongues but never talk.

🧦 Stocking

Rhyme: Hung up with care for Christmas Eve, reach in the stocking, the clue's up its sleeve!
Riddle: I'm a giant sock that nobody wears. Someone with a beard fills me once a year.

🚪 Front door mat

Rhyme: You go out through me and come back in. Look under the mat and let the search begin!
Riddle: I have a knob but no nose, and people knock on me before they come in.

🧸 Toy box

Rhyme: Teddies and blocks and cars that zoom: look in the place where the toys have room!
Riddle: I'm a chest with no heart, full of things that love to play.

🪞 Mirror

Rhyme: Who's that smiling and looking at you? Check by the mirror for your next clue!
Riddle: When you wave at me, I wave back, but I'm not your friend down the street.

🧥 Coat closet

Rhyme: Coats and jackets hang in a row. Open the closet, in you go!
Riddle: I'm full of hangers but there's not a single plane inside.

🥣 Cereal box

Rhyme: Crunchy and sweet, a breakfast treat: look in the box of the cereal you eat!
Riddle: Shake me in the morning and I pour out breakfast.

🪥 Toothbrush cup

Rhyme: Brush, brush, brush, the bristles go. Look by the sink: the next clue's there, you know!
Riddle: I have bristles but I'm not a hedgehog, and I visit your teeth twice a day.

🍪 Cookie jar

Rhyme: Cookies for Santa and cookies for you: peek in the jar for the very next clue!
Riddle: I'm a jar that never stays full for long, because somebody keeps sneaking treats.

📺 TV

Rhyme: Shows and cartoons on my screen appear. Look near the TV: your clue is here!
Riddle: I have a screen and a remote, and I tell stories without any pages.

🪟 Window

Rhyme: Look through the glass at the sky so wide. Your clue is waiting by the window side!
Riddle: I'm made of glass, I let the light in, and you can see the whole world through me.

🪜 Stairs

Rhyme: Up and down, step after step: check the stairs, don't skip a step!
Riddle: I go up and down all day, but I never move.

💡 Lamp

Rhyme: Click me on when the room gets dark: check by the lamp for the next clue's spark!
Riddle: I wear a shade but I'm not a tree, and I light up the room.

🪴 Plant

Rhyme: Green leaves growing in a pot of dirt: look by the plant, it won't get hurt!
Riddle: I drink water, love sunshine and never leave my pot.

🎒 Backpack

Rhyme: You take me to school with your books and a snack: unzip the zipper of your backpack!
Riddle: I ride on your back to school and carry all your stuff.

🐾 Pet bed

Rhyme: Where our furry friend curls up to snore, look in their bed: there's a clue for sure!
Riddle: It's a cozy bed for someone with four legs and a tail.

📬 Mailbox

Rhyme: Letters and cards come here every day. Open it up: your clue's on its way!
Riddle: I eat letters every day but I'm never hungry.

🚗 Car

Rhyme: Vroom vroom, but we're not going far: look inside the family car!
Riddle: I have four wheels and two headlight eyes, and I drive you to school.
